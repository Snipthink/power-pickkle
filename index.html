Frequently asked questions

Is it really boneless?

Yes. We use boneless fish, cut into small paneer-like cubes. As with any natural fish product, chew normally and supervise young children.

Why do the pieces look like paneer?

We cut the fish into neat cubes instead of fish-shaped pieces, so every bite is even, easy to eat and fully coated in masala.

Do I need to cook or heat it?

No. Power Pickkle is ready to eat. Open the lid, take a spoonful with a clean dry spoon, and enjoy it with chapati, paratha, rice, dal or khichdi.

How should I store it and how long does it last?

Best before 3 months from packaging when stored as per instructions on the pack.

Refrigerate after opening and consume as directed on the pack.
